# Hash Race

**Hash Race** is a 2D Bitcoin mining strategy simulation about winning the hardware race. The long-term design combines empire strategy, property/deal making, hardware evolution, facility management, life-sim choices, and sports-franchise-style seasons, ratings, rivalries, budgets, partnerships, standings, legacy, and dynasty goals.

## Current playable loop

Choose 1 of 10 fictional technology/mining companies, each with a different starting advantage. Then manage the company through a time-based mining race:

1. Run miners and earn simulated Bitcoin mining revenue.
2. Pay electricity and operating costs.
3. Buy ASICs while staying inside facility power limits.
4. Expand site capacity when infrastructure becomes the bottleneck.
5. Fund R&D and evolve through new ASIC generations.
6. Sign AI, robotics, energy, semiconductor, finance, infrastructure, real-estate, quick-service, telecom, or sports partnerships.
7. Track franchise OVR, seasonal league position, rivals, company value, and hashrate.
8. Acquire weaker rival companies and absorb their mining capacity.
9. Progress from TH/s hardware toward PH/s and eventually EH/s-class systems.

## Gameplay direction

Hash Race is being designed as an original game, not a copy of any existing franchise. Useful high-level design influences include long-form technology progression, economic negotiation and acquisition, collectible/evolving hardware, facility and people management, and sports franchise-management systems such as seasons, standings, ratings, staff, budgets, rivalries, progression, and dynasty goals.

The main visual project is built in **Unity + C#**. Helpful implementation references include the [Unity Manual](https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/index.html), [Unity 2D documentation](https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/Unity2D.html), and Microsoft’s [.NET deployment documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/dotnet/core/deploying/), which is used for the downloadable self-contained desktop prototype.

## Mining metrics used by the simulation

- Hashrate in TH/s, PH/s, and EH/s
- Machine efficiency in J/TH
- Power draw in kW and energy use in kWh
- Electricity price in $/kWh
- Uptime
- Bitcoin price
- Network hashrate
- Block subsidy
- Estimated BTC mined per day
- Revenue, power cost, profit, cash, and company value
- R&D progress and hardware generation

The basic power formula is:

`power watts = hashrate TH/s × J/TH`

The basic mining-share model is:

`BTC/day = player hashrate ÷ network hashrate × blocks/day × block subsidy × uptime`

For Bitcoin protocol background, see the [Bitcoin Developer Guide](https://developer.bitcoin.org/devguide/) and [Bitcoin developer reference](https://developer.bitcoin.org/reference/).

## Partnerships

Partnerships are strategic systems rather than cosmetic badges:

- **AI** — faster research and compute revenue
- **Robotics** — higher uptime and lower operations cost
- **Energy** — lower electricity cost
- **Semiconductor** — lower J/TH and faster ASIC development
- **Finance** — stronger acquisition economics
- **Infrastructure** — larger sites and cheaper expansion
- **Real Estate** — cheaper property expansion and more usable site space
- **Quick Service** — recurring commercial revenue
- **Telecom** — better uptime and lower operating friction
- **Sports** — sponsorship revenue and franchise prestige

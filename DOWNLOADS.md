## v0.003 gameplay change

Ending a quarterly turn now uses a two-click confirmation. The first click shows the projected quarter cash profit or loss using the current BTC price, fees, uptime, electricity cost, hosting revenue, and treasury-sale setting. The second click advances the roughly 91-day quarter. This reduces accidental turn skips and makes the most important recurring decision easier to understand before committing it.

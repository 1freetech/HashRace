# Dynamic Company Personality System

Hash Race companies are fictional Bitcoin miners. Each miner now begins with a company history, a past controversy, a strategic posture, and seven 0–100 culture ratings.

The ratings are **Aggression, Risk, Growth, R&D, Treasury, Operations, and Reputation**. A company is labeled Aggressive, Moderate, or Conservative from its current aggression and risk profile. These values are starting templates rather than permanent classes.

Player ratings change from actual player decisions. Buying more machines, expanding MW or land, taking or repaying debt, changing the BTC treasury policy, advancing chip research, and signing partnerships can move the company culture over time.

Rival ratings change from performance and AI decisions. Each in-game month, rivals compare expansion, R&D, infrastructure, and treasury strategies. Their current ratings influence which choice wins. Rivals can also sign partnerships that match their company history.

Controversies are dynamic. Higher aggression and risk create a larger chance of a new permitting, financing, deployment, or local power-use dispute. Controversies can reduce cash and reputation while pushing a company toward better operations or stronger treasury discipline afterward.

The company setup screen shows the original history, founding controversy, starting posture, and all seven ratings. During play, the company sidebar shows the player's current culture. Opening the player HQ or any rival company shows its history, current ratings, latest action, partnership state, and most recent controversy.

The system runs on elapsed game time rather than button presses. A day turn therefore does not change company culture as fast as a month or quarter turn. Roughly every 30.4375 elapsed days produces one company-behavior cycle, preserving the existing flexible day/week/month/quarter clock.
